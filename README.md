🧠 Memory Game – Test Your Brain Power!

A fun and challenging mental game to boost your memory and focus. The goal is simple: flip the cards and find all matching pairs in the fewest attempts and fastest time.

Game Features:

· 🎯 8 pairs of randomly arranged numbers each round.
· ⏱️ Timer to track your completion time.
· 📊 Accurate attempt counter.
· 🏆 Best score saved in your browser (attempts + time).
· 🌙 Modern, eye-catching design that works on all devi
ces.

Who is this game for?
Perfect for everyone – kids to develop memory, adults to sharpen their minds, and for family challenges and competitions.

How to Play:

1. Flip two cards at a time.
2. If they match – they stay revealed.
3. If they don’t match – they flip back .

4. Try to complete all pairs with the fewest attempts and fastest time.

Get ready for the challenge and set your new record!
